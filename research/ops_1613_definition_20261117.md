# OPS 1-613: verified 2025 definition and interpretation limits

Verified against the official BfArM OPS Version 2025 on 17 November 2026.

Source: https://klassifikationen.bfarm.de/ops/kode-suche/htmlops2025/block-1-61...1-69.htm

The official designation of OPS 1-613 is “Evaluation des Schluckens mit flexiblem Endoskop”. It excludes diagnostic laryngoscopy (1-610), diagnostic pharyngoscopy (1-611), and early rehabilitation assessment in patients with head and neck tumors (1-775).

The explicit rule that FEES is not separately coded under 1-613 appears within the 1-775 head-and-neck-tumor assessment definition:

Source: https://klassifikationen.bfarm.de/ops/kode-suche/htmlops2025/block-1-70...1-79.htm

That assessment combines functional evaluation in at least two of five specified domains. Its swallowing-assessment component may involve FEES or videofluoroscopy. The rule is specific to 1-775 and cannot be generalized to all neurological early rehabilitation under 8-552 or all acute cerebrovascular complex care.

Implication for these manuscripts: OPS 1-613 identifies documented flexible endoscopic swallowing evaluation, not all dysphagia assessment or all eligible patients.
